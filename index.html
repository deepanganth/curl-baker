The story

Baked from a kitchen table, still is.

The Curl Baker started with one tray of over-swirled cinnamon cookies and a lot of trial and error on how far dough can curl before it snaps. Every recipe since has chased that same edge — thin enough to crisp at the corners, thick enough to stay soft in the middle.

No factory lines, no shortcuts. Just a small kitchen, small batches, and cookies that come out a little different every day — the way anything hand-rolled should.
